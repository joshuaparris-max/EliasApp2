## Parent-approved links

These links are maintained here for parent review. They are not currently displayed in the child-facing EliasApp UI.

### Web games to review

- [Toy Theater Build](https://toytheater.com/build/) - simple browser block building for early stacking and arranging.
- [Toy Theater](https://toytheater.com/) - broader educational browser games; parent should choose safe activities first.
- [CrazyGames Building Games](https://www.crazygames.com/t/build) - portal with many building games; parent supervision required.
- [Code.org](https://code.org/) - future visual coding option for older kids.
- [ScratchJr](https://www.scratchjr.org/) - future creative coding option for preschoolers and older.

### Apps to review

- [LEGO DUPLO World on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/lego-duplo-world/id1458749093) - colourful DUPLO-style building and toddler-friendly play.
- [LEGO DUPLO World on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.storytoys.lego.duplo.world.kids.play.free.friends.animals.androidgoogleplay) - Android version of the same experience.
- [Toca Boca World official page](https://www.tocaboca.com/app/world/) - open-ended pretend play world with room and scene building.
- [Toca Boca World on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.tocaboca.tocalifeworld) - Android listing; review in-app purchases/settings first.
- [Dr. Panda Trucks on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/dr-panda-trucks/id1226883811) - construction trucks, cranes, and building play.

## Notes for EliasApp design

- Best inspiration: Toy Theater Build, LEGO DUPLO World, and Dr. Panda Trucks.
- Keep game portals out of child-facing UI unless a dedicated parent-only page is added.
- Use big touch targets, no autoplay, simple cause/effect, visual feedback, and parent-controlled content.
